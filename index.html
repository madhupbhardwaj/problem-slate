    Straight lines

01

Natural ink

Round and chisel pens respond to pressure from supported pen tablets. A translucent highlighter builds up with each pass.

Mark it up

Paste or drop images, move and resize them, then write or highlight on top. The eraser only removes ink.

A white board with a dot grid, or switch to black and your black ink turns white automatically.

Install on Mac

On your Mac.
Nothing to build.

  1. 1

    Download and unzip

    Download for Mac. Safari unzips it for you; otherwise double-click the ZIP in Finder.

  2. 2

    Move it to Applications

    Drag problem-slate.app into your Applications folder.

  3. 3

    Open it the first time

    The app isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks first. On macOS 15 or later, open it once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. On macOS 13–14, Control-click the app, choose Open, then Open again.

Good to know

A universal app for Apple silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 13 or later. It's compiled and its automated tests pass on a macOS build machine, but this release hasn't yet been hands-on tested on a Mac or with pen tablets. Updating from 1.6? Your .slate boards and recovery data carry over.

Install on Windows

The same tools.
Nothing to build.

  1. 1

    Download and extract

    Download for Windows, then right-click the ZIP and choose Extract All.

  2. 2

    Open problem-slate.exe

    Double-click problem-slate.exe in the extracted folder. Everything it needs is inside the EXE.

  3. 3

    If Windows asks

    The app isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC". Choose More info, then Run anyway.

Preview status

Version 1.7 for Windows 10/11 x64 passes its automated board tests on a Windows build machine, but hasn't yet been hands-on tested on Windows PCs or with pen tablets. Windows boards use .wslate and aren't interchangeable with Mac .slate files. Export or copy a PNG to share between them.
